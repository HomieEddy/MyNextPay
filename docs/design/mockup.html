NextPay, Wallet-style

One big number you can trust, a stack of cards for the details, and sheets that slide up when you need more. Light only, for now.

Try it

  • Tap any card in the stack to open its sheet.
  • Tap the orange plus to log an expense and watch the number move.
  • Tick bills off in the Bills sheet.
  • Use the arrows to flip between pay cycles, or open Cycles below.
  • Calendar: tap a day, or switch to Week.
  • Recurring: search, filter, tap an item to edit its schedule.
  • Settings: raise the floor above $858 and a warning appears on Home.

First run

A short setup: password, pay, tracking mode, balance (Ledger only), bills, floor, then the reveal of your first safe-to-spend number. Finishing fills the app with your answers. Erase everything in Settings brings you back here.

Two ways to track

Simple: each payday starts fresh. Paycheck minus that cycle's bills is your number. Bills log themselves, and the + is only for big surprises like a car repair. Ledger: starts from your real bank balance and you log every expense. You pick in onboarding and can switch in Settings.

Inter throughout, with tabular figures so amounts line up and don't jiggle as they count.

Motion

  • The safe-to-spend number counts to its value.
  • The days-left ring fills on open.
  • Cards rise in, then lift when you hover or press.
  • Sheets spring up from the bottom.
  • Bills tick off with a small pop.

All motion switches off when the phone asks for reduced motion.
